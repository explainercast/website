{%- comment -%} Schema.org JSON-LD. Always emits the PodcastSeries + WebSite entities. The homepage additionally emits every episode as a PodcastEpisode; an episode page emits just its own. Durations in post front matter are written MM:SS (values over an hour are carried in the minutes field, e.g. "71:02"), so they convert to PT{M}M{S}S. Anything that does not split on ":" is skipped rather than guessed at. {%- endcomment -%}
